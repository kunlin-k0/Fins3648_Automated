# STYLE.md — single-voice style guide

Every skill loads this before writing. Non-negotiable: the report is marked on reading
as *one document*, not five.

## Language and register
- **Australian English** (organisation, analyse, centre, licence [noun] / license [verb]).
- Third person. No "we think", "we believe", "I". Use "this proposal", "the solution".
- Present tense for what the solution does; future for what it will do post-funding.
- Plain, declarative sentences. No consultant filler: avoid "leverage synergies",
  "paradigm shift", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "cutting-edge".

## Citations
- One format throughout. **Default: Harvard author–date** — `(Breidbach et al., 2022)`.
- The convenor confirmed any common format is acceptable *provided it is consistent*.
- Do not over-cite. One citation per claim is enough.
- Full reference list at the end; references do not count toward the 25 pages.

## Structure conventions
- Sentence case headings ("Proposed solution", not "Proposed Solution").
- Maximum three heading levels.
- Every figure and table numbered and referred to in the body text.
- Bullet lists: maximum six items, parallel grammatical structure.

## Numbers
- Australian dollars, written `A$1.2m`, `A$450,000`.
- Percentages to one decimal at most.
- Every number carries a source or an `[ASSUMPTION: ...]` tag (CLAUDE.md Rule 2).
