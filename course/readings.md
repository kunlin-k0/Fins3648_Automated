# Extended materials index

Course lectures are the primary source; these supplement. Note what each is *good for* —
citing a reading for the wrong purpose reads as padding.

| Reading | Best used for |
|---|---|
| *How Everything-as-a-Service Enabled Judo to Become a Billion-Dollar Bank Without Owning IT* (Breidbach, Joshi, Maglio, von Briel, Twigg, Dickens, Wünderlich — MIS Quarterly Executive 21:3, Sept 2022) | The authoritative Judo source. EaaS benefits, three risk classes, execution recommendations |
| *Case Study: How Kabbage Revolutionized Business Loans* | Alternative credit scoring; automation trade-offs; the downside |
| *DBS Bank: Transformation to the Best Digital/Bank in the World* + INSEAD Knowledge piece | Incumbent transformation, culture, sequencing |
| *Inside Ping An* (UNSW Fintech Industry Sharing) | Ecosystem strategy, incumbent AI adoption |
| *Get Set for Open Banking* / *Customer Data Access & Open Banking* | CDR, data portability, consent, open banking commercials |
| *Toward an Integrated Technology Operating Model* / *Building Next-Gen Operating Model* | Operating model design — pairs with W2 architecture alignment |
| *Regulating AI in Finance: Human in the Loop* | W7 governance, HITL as a regulatory answer |
| *Navigating the Future: AI in Investment Management* / *Digital Wealth Manager of Today* | Wealth/investment use cases; hyper-personalisation |
| *Sentiment Analysis* / *Chat Finance* (Dowling & Lucey, Finance Research Letters 53, 2023) | NLP on unstructured data; LLM output evaluation methodology |
| *Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work* | Productivity evidence — use for quantified benefit claims |
| *AI, Investment Decisions and Inequality* | Distributional/ethical counter-argument. Valuable for the risk section |
| *Attention Is All You Need*; *DeepSeekMath* | Background only. Do **not** cite in a business proposal unless making a specific architectural point |

## Standing warning
The last two rows are research papers, not business evidence. Citing transformer papers to
justify a product decision is the "AI does magic" failure in a smarter costume.
