# Week 1 — Introduction to the World of Fintech

## Definition (examinable)
Fintech = **new/innovative technologies applied to improve financial services**. Two modes:
- **Business as usual** — new tech makes existing operations better; business model unchanged.
- **Business model transformation** — new tech creates an entirely new way of doing business
  between stakeholders.

## The four waves
| Wave | Period | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Fintech 1.0 | 1866–1967 | Analog→digital; operational efficiency in traditional banks. Markers: 1967 TI financial calculator, Barclays ATM |
| Fintech 2.0 | 1968–2008 | International financial communications infrastructure. Markers: BACS, CHIPS, SWIFT, Nasdaq (1968–71); Bloomberg (1981); Wells Fargo online checking (1995); digital-only banks (2005) |
| Fintech 3.0 | 2008–present | Consumer-facing: mobile banking, P2P lending, DeFi. Rise of fintech **and techfin** platforms on data and analytics |
| Current wave | 2025+ | AI-native finance — automated decisioning in lending, fraud detection, personalised planning |

## Crisis-driven evolution (a favourite exam angle)
Fintech evolves as a **response to crises**, not just to technology:
- **1987 Black Monday** — algo trading and portfolio insurance amplified the crash → circuit-breaker RegTech, risk assessment tech, enhanced trading algos, alternative assets.
- **1995 Barings collapse** — Nick Leeson's unauthorised derivatives trading → trade monitoring RegTech, front/back-office separation, straight-through trade flows, data flow visualisation.
- **2008 GFC** — housing bubble, MBS collapse → QE let fintechs thrive; digital banking rose; incumbents disrupted; regulatory risk adaptation.
- **2019–20 COVID** — accelerated digital payments, contactless, lending during lockdown → mass adoption, inclusion and resilience.
- **2020 Robinhood user death** — 20-year-old died by suicide after mistakenly believing he had incurred massive debt shown in-app → responsible technology, customer education, better platform design and support.

**The point:** technology adoption follows failure. When your proposal argues "why now",
crisis/regulatory triggers are stronger evidence than technology availability alone.

## Current trend list (W1)
Superapps, tokenisation, DeFi, **agentic AI**. Fintech 100 takeaways: AI agents tackling
specialised financial workflows (debt collection to autonomous payments); AI automating the
core of enterprise finance as financial operations platforms become full-stack ecosystems;
digital asset firms enabling crypto in everyday and institutional transactions.

## The success formula (directly reusable in the capstone)
All successful fintechs start by **recognising a pain point**, coupled with:
innovative solution → sound market positioning → best-fit team → pathway to scale
profitably → ability to gain track record, mitigate business and regulatory risk,
secure resources, and **pivot quickly** as needed.
