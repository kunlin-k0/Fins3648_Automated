# Week 4 — Payments, Wallets, and Embedded Finance

## The framing line
**"Payments are plumbing, products and platforms."** Small optimisations in speed and
customer experience generate significant P&L impact. Use this when arguing that a modest
efficiency gain has outsized financial consequence.

## Real-time payment rails
Immediate clearing and settlement. Consequences: better liquidity management, better
forecasting, better working capital — and **new business models** that were impossible
when settlement took days.

## Digital wallets
- Securely digitise payment methods; transforming commerce.
- Evolution: **transaction-centric → asset-centric**.
- Security via **tokenisation** and **biometric authentication**.
- Consider wallet models, **super-apps**, platform dynamics, and the UX/trust trade-off.

## Embedded finance
Integration of banking, payment, lending and insurance **into non-financial apps**,
enhancing the customer journey and loyalty. Platform finance extends this further.

## The three business models to name
- **BNPL** — embedded lending
- **BaaS** (Banking-as-a-Service) — embedded banking
- **PaaS** (Payments-as-a-Service) — embedded payment

Collectively these shift focus from traditional banking to **customer-centric, API-driven
financial services**. Assess each on monetisation strategy, operational risk and
regulatory touchpoints.
