## Task 2/3 — Demo Day Pitch

- Visual PowerPoint deck, typically **10–12 slides**.
- **5 minutes**, live and in person. Video pitches are not accepted.
- Structure: **hook → problem → solution → market opportunity → business model → team → the ask**.
- Prioritise **storytelling over detailed data**. Spark interest; do not present a full business plan.
- Heavy on visuals, little text — audience should watch the speaker, not read slides.
- **Each team member speaks a similar amount of time.**
- The deck must flow as a single presentation with consistent narrative and language,
  even though different members build different slides.

### The hook
Open with a quantified, bottom-line-up-front benefit. Convenor's examples:
"Enable 70% savings on…", "Improve customer access by 2-fold…",
"Enhance market prediction accuracy by 50%…". Purpose is to hold attention, not to prove.

## Task 4 — 25-page proposal

- Word document, **no more than 25 pages including the title page**.
- Appendices and references do **not** count toward the 25 pages. Push prototype
  screenshots, diagrams, user journeys and code into appendices.
- Must include, among other sections:
  - **Introduction** — big picture narrowing to the specific problem addressed.
  - **Proposed solution** — the solution; evidence and thought logic behind it;
    business model; technology building blocks; challenges, risks and mitigation steps.
  - **Conclusion** — summary of the problem and the proposed solution.
- Expectation of roughly **5 pages of ownership per member** in a 5-person team, but the
  document must read as one, not as five stitched together.
- Citation format: any common format, **used consistently**. Do not over-cite.
- Must be professional: this simulates competing for real external funding.
- Implementation must be **realistically achievable**, not futuristic.

## Financial evidence expected

The convenor expects a supporting spreadsheet showing:
- unit cost of the technology solution
- a P&L showing revenue streams minus costs
- a **5-year** trend (3 years often too short to reach break-even)
- break-even point and ROI
- effect of scale economies on the curve

## Two framing options (pick one, state it)

1. **Entrepreneur** — a fintech startup pitching to external investors (angel/VC).
2. **Intrapreneur** — innovators inside a major bank or FI pitching to senior management
   or the board, solving an internal inefficiency or revenue gap
   (i.e. reduce cost, or increase revenue and profitability).

## Task 1 rubric (for reference)

4 points requires: submitted on time **and** all four components — roles of members,
key milestones, target dates, who is responsible for each milestone — realistic,
detailed and well documented. Late submission caps the score at 2 points regardless
of quality.

## Topic changes
Teams may change topic after submitting the project plan; the plan governs process,
not subject matter. The convenor recommended carrying 2–3 candidate topics and moving
fast if switching late.
